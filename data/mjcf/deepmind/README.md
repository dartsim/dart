# DeepMind Control Suite MJCF samples

Source: [google-deepmind/dm_control](https://github.com/google-deepmind/dm_control),
commit [`437b7a6a416715d9ed9e419cb40060c862de3ab0`](https://github.com/google-deepmind/dm_control/tree/437b7a6a416715d9ed9e419cb40060c862de3ab0).
The XML files come from `dm_control/suite/`; `LICENSE` is the upstream repository
license, Apache-2.0. Copyright and attribution remain with the upstream authors.
These are parser fixtures, without Control Suite task logic or controllers.
DART does not implement all MuJoCo settings, actuators, sensors or materials.

## DART changes

All files are byte-for-byte upstream copies except:

- `acrobot.xml`, `pendulum.xml`, `quadruped.xml` and `swimmer.xml`: put the
  mass-bearing geom before zero-mass decoration in the affected bodies
  (upper arm, pole, torso, and swimmer head/segments respectively). DART
  currently infers a body's mass from only its first geom. Reordering leaves
  MuJoCo's mass properties unchanged and avoids massless articulated links.

- `quadruped.xml`: remove the `terrain` heightfield asset and geom. The suite's
  flat floor and four boundary walls remain; DART's MJCF loader does not support
  heightfields. Tendon coupling is also unsupported; this fixture checks free
  joints, nested defaults, stacked hip hinges and body hierarchy.
- `swimmer.xml`: add `segment_0` and `segment_1`, each at `0 .1 0` with a
  an inertial geom, visual geom, site and a hinge ranging from -120 to 120 degrees.
  These are the three-link topology generated by the pinned suite's
  `swimmer.py::_make_model(3)` and `_make_body`. The original head-only template
  has no articulated chain. Cameras, actuators and sensors are left as in the
  template; the fixture tests the root joint stack and two segment hinges.

Each modified XML file carries a DART modification notice at the top.

No model was taken from the separate MuJoCo repository.

## Test coverage and migration

Expected topology follows the XML, including DART's massless carrier bodies
for mixed or stacked hinge joints; it is not measured from parser output.

| Former sample | Replacement | XML oracle and retained coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ant.xml` | `quadruped.xml` | 17 torso-tree bodies, four hip carriers, 16 hinges plus a free root (22 DOFs); separate free ball, five root geoms and a target site (8 skeletons). |
| `inverted_pendulum.xml` | `cartpole.xml` | Cart and pole, slide plus hinge (2 DOFs), three root geoms (4 skeletons); slider limits -1.8 to 1.8, masses 1 and 0.1. |
| `inverted_double_pendulum.xml` | `acrobot.xml` | Two serial hinges (2 DOFs), floor and target site (3 skeletons); each link has mass 1. The slide-root case remains covered by cartpole. |
| `reacher.xml` | `reacher.xml` | Three arm bodies, two hinges and a welded finger; seven root geoms (8 skeletons), wrist limits -160 to 160 degrees. Also used by the Python DOF-ownership regression. |
| `humanoid.xml`, `humanoidstandup.xml` | `humanoid.xml` | 16 real bodies plus 9 carriers, 21 hinges plus free root (27 DOFs); floor (2 skeletons). Tested with DART and default collision detectors. |
| `walker2d.xml` | `walker.xml` | Seven real bodies plus 2 root carriers, 9 DOFs, floor (2 skeletons). |
| `hopper.xml` | `hopper.xml` | Five real bodies plus 2 root carriers, 7 DOFs, floor (2 skeletons). |
| `half_cheetah.xml` | `cheetah.xml` | Seven real bodies plus 2 root carriers, 9 DOFs, ground (2 skeletons). |
| `swimmer.xml` | `swimmer.xml` | Three real bodies plus 2 root carriers, 5 DOFs, ground and target (3 skeletons); each segment's inertial geom has mass 0.01. |
| `striker.xml`, `thrower.xml`, Fetch scenes | `../test/manipulation/scene.xml` | DART-authored BSD include/mesh/hierarchy fixture, six skeletons, hinge/slide/free/weld joints, visual-only markers, inherited and overridden friction, and unchanged C++/direct-Python FCL default. |

The old `pusher.xml` had no test consumer; its manipulation primitives are
covered by the authored scene. `point.xml` likewise had no consumers. The entire former model tree, including Fetch meshes and
textures, is removed. `pendulum.xml` adds a one-hinge, mass-1 smoke test.
